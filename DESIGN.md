# Lunéa visual direction

Editorial wellness: warm ivory, soft sage, muted olive and restrained brass accents. Self-hosted Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans fonts (installed via Fontsource), original local spa imagery, bespoke line-art lotus, asymmetric photo compositions and quiet motion with reduced-motion support.

Inspiration consulted (layout and mood only, no third-party images copied):
